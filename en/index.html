FAQ

Before you start

What is orphan sponsorship?

A core programme of the Society: you commit to a monthly amount that is paid to an orphan registered with it, through their own account or electronic card.

How much is it, and for how long?

OMR 25 a month for one orphan. The minimum period is one year, according to the Society's published profile.

How do I pay?

By credit or debit card on the official sponsorship page (Bank Muscat SmartPay gateway), through the Bahjah app, the Jood platform, or a bank standing-order form (Bank Dhofar or Bank Muscat).

Is there an app?

Yes — the Bahjah app lets you sponsor, donate and follow projects and urgent cases.

App Store Google Play

Are there other sponsorship options?

Per the Society's profile: food rations OMR 35, allowance plus food rations OMR 60, and health sponsorship (Zamzam) OMR 10 a month. To register, contact the Sponsors Department.
