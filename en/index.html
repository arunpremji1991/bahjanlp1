Questions, answered

Orphan sponsorship FAQ

What does it mean to sponsor an orphan?

Orphan sponsorship is one of Bahjah's core programmes. You commit to a monthly amount, and it is paid to an orphan registered with the Society through their own account or electronic card — protecting the child's privacy and dignity.

How much does sponsorship cost?

OMR 25 a month for one orphan. According to the Society's published profile, the minimum sponsorship period is one year.

Why should I sponsor an orphan?

Caring for orphans holds a special place in Islam. The Prophet ﷺ said: "I and the one who sponsors an orphan will be in Paradise like this," gesturing with his index and middle fingers. Your sponsorship is an act of mercy today and, by Allah's permission, an opportunity for reward.

How can I sponsor?

By credit or debit card on Bahjah's official sponsorship page (Bank Muscat SmartPay gateway), through the Bahjah app, the Jood platform, or a bank standing-order form (Bank Dhofar or Bank Muscat).

Can I donate more than OMR 25?

Yes. You can give further through the Society's official donations page, which includes options such as sadaqah, relief of hardship and zakat.

Is there a Bahjah app?

Yes. The Bahjah app lets you sponsor, donate and follow the Society's projects and urgent cases.

App Store Google Play

Are there other sponsorship options?

According to the Society's profile: food rations OMR 35, allowance plus food rations OMR 60, and health sponsorship (Zamzam project) OMR 10 a month. To register for these, please contact the Sponsors Department.
